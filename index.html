What

GiveMeAFuckingJ*b searches the job sites you use for roles that match your profile, then completes and submits each application for you, with your résumé attached and every question answered. It can also prepare personal emails to recruiters, which are sent only after you approve them.

The assumption

Suppose that any single application has a one-in-a-thousand chance of leading to an offer. On its own, one application is very unlikely to succeed.

02

A thousand chances

02

A 63% probability of at least one offer.

The result

If 1,000 such applications are independent, the probability that none of them succeeds is (1 − 0.001)1000, or about 36.8%. The probability of at least one offer is therefore about 63.2%.
