Privacy Policy

Effective 29 September 2026. How GMAFJ, also known as GiveMeAFuckingJ*b, collects, uses, shares and protects personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how GiveMeAFuckingJ*b ("GMAFJ", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use our desktop application, our website at givemeafuckingjob.com (the "Website"), and the online services that support them (together, the "Service").

The application is published on the Microsoft Store under the name "GMAFJ" and is distributed from the Website under the name "GiveMeAFuckingJ*b". Both are the same application (the "App"), and this policy applies to both.

By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Please also read our Terms of Use and our AI Disclosure, which explain the rules for using the Service and how it uses artificial intelligence.

1. Summary

  • The App searches for jobs, applies to them, fills in your profiles on job sites and prepares emails for you, using the information you give it.
  • To do this, it sends the relevant parts of your information to our servers, to third-party AI providers, and to the job sites, employers and people you choose to contact.
  • Your sign-ins to job sites and email accounts are stored on your device. We do not receive the passwords for those accounts.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • You can access, correct or delete your information by contacting us at the address in Section 15.

2. Information We Collect

(a) Information you provide

  • Account information: your name, email address and password. We store your password only in hashed form.
  • Profile information: contact details, location, education, work history, skills, languages, certifications, awards, links, job preferences (such as job titles, locations, salary expectations and working arrangements), work authorization, and similar details that job applications ask for.
  • Optional sensitive information: some employers ask about characteristics such as gender, disability status, veteran status or ethnicity. If you choose to provide these details, we process them only to answer those employers' questions on your behalf. You may leave them blank.
  • Résumés and documents: résumés and other files you add to the App or that the App builds for you.
  • Communications: messages you send us, such as support requests and feedback.

(b) Information created when you use the Service

  • Application activity: the searches you run, the jobs found, the applications submitted, the questions asked by job sites and employers, the answers given, and their status. This information is stored mainly on your device.
  • AI requests and responses: the questions and context sent to AI providers and the answers they return, as described in Section 5 and in our AI Disclosure.
  • Email activity: the people you choose to contact, the drafts prepared for you, and the messages you approve and send.

(c) Information collected automatically

  • Device and connection information: IP address, device type, operating system, App version, and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Session and security information: sign-in times, the devices signed in to your account, and records needed to prevent abuse.
  • Service logs: records of requests to our servers, such as their time and outcome. Our logs are designed to exclude the content of your profile and of your AI requests.
  • Diagnostic logs: crash and error logs are stored on your device and are not sent to us unless you choose to share them.

(d) Information from third parties

  • Job sites: when the App works on a job site using your sign-in, it reads the information that site shows you, such as job postings, application forms, your existing profile on that site and your account name, so that it can fill in forms correctly and avoid duplicate entries.
  • Public information about people you contact: when you use the email feature, the App may read publicly available information about the people you choose to contact, such as their name, role, employer and public professional profile, to address and personalize your message.
  • Advertising partners: our ad partners may collect information through cookies and similar technologies, as described in Section 8.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the Service: to search for jobs, fill in and submit applications and job-site profiles, answer application questions, build your résumé, and prepare the emails you approve.
  • To personalize what the Service produces: answers, cover letters, profile summaries and email drafts based on your information and preferences.
  • To keep your profile the same on every device you sign in on.
  • To create and maintain your account, verify your email address, detect and prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access, and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To send you service messages, such as verification codes, security notices and important changes to the Service.
  • To operate and improve the Service: to check for updates, show service notices, troubleshoot problems and improve reliability and features.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the Service free.
  • To comply with the law and respond to lawful requests.

4. How We Share Information

We share personal information only as described below.

(a) At your direction

The App sends your information to the job sites and employers you apply to or keep a profile with, as part of each application or profile it completes for you. It sends the emails you approve to their recipients through your own email account. Those parties handle the information under their own privacy policies.

(b) Service providers

We use third parties to help us run the Service, including providers of hosting and infrastructure, network security and delivery, AI models, and email delivery for account messages, and, if paid plans are introduced, payment processing. They receive only the information they need to perform their services.

(c) Advertising partners

Our ad partners receive information through cookies and similar technologies, as described in Section 8.

(d) Legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information to comply with applicable law, legal process or enforceable government requests; to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us; and to enforce our Terms of Use.

(e) Business transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

(f) With your consent

We may share information in other ways when you ask us to or agree to it.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some laws treat the use of cookies for personalized advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; see Sections 8 and 10.

5. Artificial Intelligence

  • The App uses third-party AI models to write answers to application questions, cover letters, profile summaries and email drafts, and to help it operate the pages of job sites.
  • For each task, it sends the relevant information, such as the question being asked, the options offered, details about the job or the recipient, and the relevant parts of your profile, to our servers, which pass it to an AI provider.
  • To keep answers consistent, the AI provider may keep a conversation linked to your account that contains your profile information and earlier questions.
  • AI providers process this information under their own terms and may retain it. Their terms may allow them to use it to improve their services. Do not add information to your profile that you do not want processed in this way.

Our AI Disclosure explains what the AI does, its limits, and your responsibilities.

6. Storage, Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: your application history, saved answers, résumés, settings, and your sign-ins to job sites and email accounts remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App and its data. Stored secrets are protected with your operating system's encryption.
  • On our servers: we keep your account and profile while your account is open. AI requests are kept only as long as needed to deliver the answers, normally no longer than 24 hours. Verification codes are deleted within a day. Session and security records are kept for as long as they are needed for security.
  • Backups: copies of server data may remain in backups for a limited period before they are overwritten.
  • Account deletion: when you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymize your account and profile data in our active systems, except information we must keep to comply with the law, resolve disputes or prevent fraud. Information already submitted to job sites, employers, email recipients or AI providers is held by them under their own policies, and we cannot delete it for you.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, encryption of stored profiles on our servers, hashed passwords, operating-system protection for secrets stored on your device, and limited access to our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your device and your account credentials. Please report any security concern to the address in Section 15.

8. Advertising, Cookies and Similar Technologies

  • The App and the Website show ads served by third-party ad partners, including Google. These partners may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often they appear, measure their performance and, where permitted, personalize them.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our sites and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn about other choices at aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Where the law requires consent for these technologies, we ask for it before they are used, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • The App's built-in browser stores cookies for the job sites and email accounts you sign in to, so that you stay signed in. These are kept on your device and used only to operate those sites for you.
  • We do not use third-party analytics tools in the App.

9. Your Choices

  • You can view and edit your profile, and review the answers the App has saved, in the App.
  • You can stop a job search, an application run or a profile update at any time.
  • No email is sent until you approve it.
  • You can see every device signed in to your account and sign any of them out in the App.
  • You can manage personalized advertising as described in Section 8.
  • You can stop using the Service at any time, and ask us to delete your account and data.
