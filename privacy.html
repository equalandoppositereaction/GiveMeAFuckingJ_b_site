Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026. How GMAFJ collects, uses, shares and protects personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how GMAFJ ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use our desktop application, our website at givemeafuckingjob.com and the GMAFJ social network on it (the "Website"), and the online services that support them (together, the "Service").

The application is published on the Microsoft Store under the name "GMAFJ" (the "App").

By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Please also read our Terms of Use and our AI Disclosure, which explain the rules for using the Service and how it uses artificial intelligence.

1. Summary

  • The Website is a professional social network for getting a job. You have a profile, share updates, connect with and follow people, message your connections, and search and apply to jobs.
  • Your profile and the updates you share are meant to be seen by other people. You choose who sees what in Settings, as Section 4 explains.
  • The App searches for jobs, applies to them, fills in your profiles on job sites and prepares emails for you, using the information you give it. Jobs you send from the Website with One click apply are applied to by the App.
  • To do this, the Service sends the relevant parts of your information to our servers, to third-party AI providers, and to the job sites, employers and people you choose to contact.
  • Your sign-ins to job sites and email accounts are stored on your device. We do not receive the passwords for those accounts.
  • Everything on the Service is free until 31 December 2026, and we do not collect payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • You can access, correct or delete your information in Settings or by contacting us at the address in Section 17.

2. Information We Collect

(a) Information you provide

  • Account information: your name, email address and password. We store your password only in hashed form. To keep out fake accounts, we accept sign-ups only from addresses at major email providers.
  • Your profile on the Website: your username, name, pronouns, photo, cover picture, headline, location, About, website, experience, education, licenses and certifications, projects, volunteering, honors and awards, languages and skills; whether you are open to work, with the roles, places and kinds of work you are looking for, or hiring; and the updates you feature.
  • Your profile design: the HTML, CSS and pictures you use to design your own profile page.
  • What you share: updates, pictures and GIFs, comments and replies, reactions, reposts, endorsements, recommendations you write, and invitations to connect with the notes you add to them.
  • Messages: the messages you send to your connections, and the updates you send in them.
  • Your network: your connections, the people you follow and block, and the content you report.
  • Your profile in the App: contact details, location, education, work history, skills, languages, certifications, awards, links, job preferences (such as job titles, locations, salary expectations and working arrangements), work authorization, and similar details that job applications ask for. You can copy parts of it into your profile on the Website with "Fill in from the GMAFJ app"; your contact details are never copied.
  • Optional sensitive information: some employers ask about characteristics such as gender, disability status, veteran status or ethnicity. If you choose to provide these details in the App, we process them only to answer those employers' questions on your behalf. You may leave them blank. They are never shown on your profile.
  • Résumés and documents: résumés and other files you add to the App or that the App builds for you.
  • Communications: messages you send us, such as support requests and feedback.

(b) Information created when you use the Service

  • Activity on the Website: the updates shown to you in your feed, the updates you open, hide or save, the profiles you view, the jobs you save, apply to or send to the App with One click apply, and your notifications.
  • Application activity: the searches the App runs, the jobs found, the applications submitted, the questions asked by job sites and employers, the answers given, and their status. This information is stored mainly on your device.
  • AI requests and responses: the questions and context sent to AI providers and the answers they return, as described in Section 7 and in our AI Disclosure.
  • Email activity: the people you choose to contact, the drafts prepared for you, and the messages you approve and send.

(c) Information collected automatically

  • Device and connection information: IP address, device type, operating system, browser, App version, and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Session and security information: sign-in times, the devices signed in to your account, and records needed to prevent abuse, such as how many invitations you send in a day.
  • Service logs: records of requests to our servers, such as their time and outcome. Our logs are designed to exclude the content of your profile, your messages and your AI requests.
  • Diagnostic logs: the App's crash and error logs are stored on your device and are not sent to us unless you choose to share them.

(d) Information from others

  • Other members: other members may provide information about you, for example by mentioning you in an update or comment, endorsing your skills, writing you a recommendation, sending you a message, or reporting content.
  • Job sites: when the App works on a job site using your sign-in, it reads the information that site shows you, such as job postings, application forms, your existing profile on that site and your account name, so that it can fill in forms correctly and avoid duplicate entries.
  • Public information about people you contact: when you use the App's email feature, the App may read publicly available information about the people you choose to contact, such as their name, role, employer and public professional profile, to address and personalize your message.
  • Advertising partners: our ad partners may collect information through cookies and similar technologies, as described in Section 10.

3. How We Use Information

  • To run the social network: to show your profile, updates and activity to others as your settings allow, deliver your messages and notifications, and let you connect with, follow, endorse and recommend people.
  • To choose what you see: we rank the updates in your "For you" feed and choose the people we suggest you connect with automatically, using signals such as your connections and the people you follow, the updates and profiles you engage with, open or hide, how recent an update is, and the companies, schools, skills and places in profiles. Your "Following" feed shows updates newest first, without ranking. These choices are not used to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.
  • To show your connections' activity: which of your connections reacted to an update, and which applied to a job through GMAFJ, unless they turned that off.
  • To provide the App: to search for jobs, fill in and submit applications and job-site profiles, answer application questions, build your résumé, prepare the emails you approve, and apply to the jobs you send it from the Website.
  • To personalize what the Service produces: answers, cover letters, profile summaries and email drafts based on your information and preferences.
  • To keep your App profile the same on every device you sign in on.
  • To keep the community safe: to review profile designs before they are shown, look into reported content, detect and prevent fraud, spam, abuse and unauthorized access, and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To create and maintain your account and verify your email address.
  • To send you service messages, such as verification codes, security notices and important changes to the Service.
  • To operate and improve the Service: to check for updates, show service notices, troubleshoot problems and improve reliability and features.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the Service free.
  • To comply with the law and respond to lawful requests.

4. What Other People Can See

  • Your profile: everyone signed in to GMAFJ can see your profile, your profile design once we approve it, and how many connections and followers you have. Unless you turn it off in Settings, visitors without an account can see it too, and it may be found through search engines.
  • Your updates: an update for "Anyone" can be seen by anyone with its link, including people without an account, and members can react to it, comment on it, repost it and send it to others. An update for "Connections only" can be seen only by you and your connections. Comments and reactions can be seen by everyone who can see the update.
  • Your activity: your connections may see in their feed that you reacted to or commented on an update. They may also see your face on a job you applied to with Apply or One click apply, unless you turn off "My connections can see the jobs I apply to"; then you are counted, never named.
  • Profile views: when you open someone's profile, they may see that you viewed it. If you turn on "View profiles privately", they see "Someone on GMAFJ" instead, and you see only how many people viewed your profile, not who.
  • Your connections: your connections can see your list of connections, unless you turn that off. Others see only how many connections you have, and how many you share with them.
  • Your email address: your connections can see it in your contact info, unless you choose "Nobody".
  • Endorsements and recommendations: the endorsements of your skills are shown on your profile. A recommendation written for you is shown only once you choose to show it. Recommendations you write are shown on the profiles of the people you recommend, with your name.
  • Messages: a message can be seen only by you and the person you sent it to, except as described in Section 6.
  • Blocking: when you block someone, neither of you can see the other's profile, updates, comments or messages on the Service, and any connection between you ends.

5. How We Share Information

We share personal information only as described below.

(a) With other members and visitors

The Service shows your profile, updates and activity to others as described in Section 4.

(b) At your direction

The App sends your information to the job sites and employers you apply to or keep a profile with, as part of each application or profile it completes for you, including the jobs you send it from the Website. It sends the emails you approve to their recipients through your own email account. Those parties handle the information under their own privacy policies.

(c) Service providers

We use third parties to help us run the Service, including providers of hosting and infrastructure, network security and delivery, AI models, and email delivery for account messages, and, when paid plans are offered, payment processing. They receive only the information they need to perform their services.

(d) Advertising partners

Our ad partners receive information through cookies and similar technologies, as described in Section 10.

(e) Legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information to comply with applicable law, legal process or enforceable government requests; to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us; and to enforce our Terms of Use.

(f) Business transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

(g) With your consent

We may share information in other ways when you ask us to or agree to it.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some laws treat the use of cookies for personalized advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; see Sections 10 and 12.

6. Moderation and Example Profiles

  • Our admins review every profile design before it is shown, and look into content that members report. To act on a report about a message, they can read that message, and only that one.
  • We may remove content and restrict accounts as our Terms of Use describe.
  • To show what a profile can look like, the Service includes example profiles made by GMAFJ. They describe fictional people, are marked "Example" wherever they appear, and cannot be connected to, followed or messaged. They are not based on any real person's personal information.

7. Artificial Intelligence

  • The App uses third-party AI models to write answers to application questions, cover letters, profile summaries and email drafts, and to help it operate the pages of job sites.
  • For each task, it sends the relevant information, such as the question being asked, the options offered, details about the job or the recipient, and the relevant parts of your profile, to our servers, which pass it to an AI provider.
  • To keep answers consistent, the AI provider may keep a conversation linked to your account that contains your profile information and earlier questions.
  • AI providers process this information under their own terms and may retain it. Their terms may allow them to use it to improve their services. Do not add information to your profile that you do not want processed in this way.
  • The Website offers a prompt you can copy into an AI tool of your own choice to design your profile page. Whatever you give that tool is handled under its own terms, not ours.

Our AI Disclosure explains what the AI does, its limits, and your responsibilities.

8. Storage, Retention and Deletion

  • On your device: the App's application history, saved answers, résumés, settings, and your sign-ins to job sites and email accounts remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App and its data. Stored secrets are protected with your operating system's encryption.
  • On our servers: we keep your account, profiles, updates, comments, messages, connections and pictures while your account is open, until you delete them. Records of the updates shown to you and opened by you are kept for 90 days, profile views for 120 days, and notifications you have read for 90 days. Pictures that nothing uses are deleted after a day, and pictures uploaded for a profile design after 30 days unused. AI requests are kept only as long as needed to deliver the answers, normally no longer than 24 hours. Verification codes are deleted within a day. Session and security records are kept for as long as they are needed for security.
  • Content you delete: when you delete an update, a comment or your profile design, we remove it from the Service. Reposts of a deleted update show that it is no longer available. Copies of a picture that networks and browsers keep to load pages faster can take up to a day to disappear.
  • Backups: copies of server data may remain in backups for a limited period before they are overwritten.
  • Account deletion: you can delete your account in Settings. We then delete your account, profiles, updates, comments, reactions, messages, connections and pictures from our active systems, except information we must keep to comply with the law, resolve disputes or prevent fraud. Information already submitted to job sites, employers, email recipients or AI providers is held by them under their own policies, and we cannot delete it for you. Other people may have saved or copied what you shared publicly before it was deleted.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, encryption of the App profiles and the messages stored on our servers, hashed passwords, a sign-in cookie that the Website's scripts cannot read, checks that every uploaded picture really is a JPEG, PNG or GIF (JPEG and PNG pictures are rewritten, which removes hidden data such as camera location), profile designs shown in a sealed frame that cannot run their own scripts, operating-system protection for secrets stored on your device, and limited access to our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your device and your account credentials. Please report any security concern to the address in Section 17.

10. Advertising, Cookies and Similar Technologies

  • When you sign in on the Website, we set a cookie that keeps you signed in until you log out or have not used the Website for 30 days. It is used only to sign you in, and the Website's scripts cannot read it.
  • The Website also keeps a few small preferences in your browser, such as which feed you last chose and whether you were signed in, so that the page shows the right menus at once. They stay on your device.
  • The App and the Website show ads served by third-party ad partners, including Google. These partners may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often they appear, measure their performance and, where permitted, personalize them.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our sites and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn about other choices at aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Where the law requires consent for these technologies, we ask for it before they are used, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • The App's built-in browser stores cookies for the job sites and email accounts you sign in to, so that you stay signed in. These are kept on your device and used only to operate those sites for you.
  • We do not use third-party analytics tools.

11. Your Choices

  • You can view and edit your profile on the Website in Settings, and your profile in the App in the App.
  • In Settings you choose whether visitors without an account can see your profile, whether your connections see the jobs you apply to, who can see your connections and your email address, and whether you view profiles privately.
  • You choose who can see each update ("Anyone" or "Connections only"), and you can edit or delete your updates and comments at any time.
  • You can hide updates you do not want to see, block people, and report content.
  • You choose whether a recommendation written for you is shown on your profile.
  • You can stop a job search, an application run or a profile update in the App at any time. No email is sent until you approve it.
  • You can see every device signed in to your account and sign any of them out in the App.
  • You can manage personalized advertising as described in Section 10.
  • You can stop using the Service at any time, and delete your account in Settings or ask us to delete it.

You may object to the ranking of your feed at any time by using the "Following" feed.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and disclose it, to access, correct and delete it, to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We use sensitive personal information only to provide the Service you request. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

(e) How to make a request

You may use an authorized agent where the law allows. If we decline your request, you may ask us to reconsider it by replying to our decision.

15. Third-Party Sites and Services

The App works on third-party websites and services, such as job sites and email providers. The jobs listed on the Website open the pages of their employers and job sites, and members' profiles, updates and designs may link to other sites. Their own privacy policies govern how they handle your information. We are not responsible for their practices.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date and, if the changes are material, tell you on the Website, in the App or by email. Your continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the updated policy.
