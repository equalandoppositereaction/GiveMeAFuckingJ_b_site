Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

What we collect

  • Account details, such as your name and email address.
  • What you add to the app: your profile, résumé, job preferences, and optional details some employers ask for, used only to answer them.
  • What the app does for you: the jobs it applies to, the answers and emails it writes, and their results.
  • Technical data, such as device, app and log information.

Your sign-ins to job sites and email accounts stay on your device.

How we use it

To provide the app, keep your profile in sync across your devices, secure and improve the service, and contact you about your account.

Who we share it with

  • The job sites, employers and people you apply to or email through the app.
  • Third parties that help us run the service, such as hosting, AI, email and advertising providers.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or a buyer if our business is sold.

We do not sell your personal information.

Ads and cookies

Our website and the app show ads from third parties, including Google, which may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

Storage and security

Your data is kept on your device and on our servers, and may be processed in other countries. We keep it while your account is open, or as long as the law requires. We protect it with reasonable safeguards, but no system is fully secure.

Your choices

You can edit your profile in the app, sign out of any device, and stop using the app at any time. To see, correct or delete your data or your account, email us.

Children

The app is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here.
