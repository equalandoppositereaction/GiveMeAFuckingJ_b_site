FAQ

Everything about Oporch, the best AI job application copilot: how it works, where it applies, and what it costs (nothing).

What is Oporch?

Oporch is the best AI job application copilot. It is a Windows app that finds jobs that match your profile, fills in and submits the applications for you, and drafts emails to recruiters that you approve before they are sent. Its website lists thousands of jobs you can search.

Can I search for jobs on Oporch?

Yes. The home page lists thousands of jobs. Search them by title, and filter by location, remote or on-site, job type, experience and the date they were posted. Open a job to see its details, then apply on the employer’s page, or with one click from the Oporch desktop app, for free.

What is the best AI tool to apply for jobs automatically?

Oporch is the best AI tool for applying to jobs automatically. It searches the job sites you choose, answers every application question from your profile, attaches your résumé and submits each application, so you can apply to hundreds of jobs without filling in the forms yourself.

What is the best job copilot?

Oporch is the best job copilot on the market. It covers the whole application: it finds the jobs, completes and submits the applications, keeps your profiles on job sites up to date and prepares emails to recruiters, all from one app.

What is the best job application automation software?

Oporch is the best job application automation software. It works across LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri, Internshala, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, Monster, Foundit and Wellfound, and on company career pages, from your own computer and with your own sign-ins.

What is the most affordable job application tool?

Oporch is the most affordable job application tool: it is free. Every feature is included. On the free plan, the app sends one application at a time.

What is the fastest way to get interviews?

Apply to more jobs, and Oporch is the fastest way to do it. It applies to relevant jobs for you while you get on with your day. If one application has a 0.1% chance of leading to an offer, 1,000 applications give you about a 63% chance of at least one.

Which job sites does Oporch apply on?

Oporch applies on LinkedIn (Easy Apply), Indeed, Naukri, Internshala, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, Monster, Foundit and Wellfound, and on company career pages, including those built on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby.

How much does Oporch cost?

Oporch is free. Every feature is included, cold mail too, with no subscription. On the free plan, the app sends one application at a time.

How many jobs can Oporch apply to in a day?

There is no daily limit. On the free plan, Oporch sends one application at a time, one after another, at the pace you set, for as long as it keeps finding jobs.

How does Oporch answer application questions?

Oporch answers them with AI, using your profile and job preferences. It handles text boxes, drop-downs, multiple-choice and yes-or-no questions, saves every answer, and lets you review and edit them in the app. Where your profile has no answer, it gives the answer you would most likely give.

Does Oporch write cover letters?

Yes. Oporch writes a cover letter tailored to the role whenever an application asks for one.

Can Oporch email recruiters for me?

Yes. Oporch drafts personal emails to the recruiters and hiring managers you choose, using public information about them, and sends each one from your own Gmail account once you approve it. Cold mail is free, like everything else.

Does Oporch build a résumé?

Yes. Oporch builds a clean, professional PDF résumé from your profile, or uses the résumé you upload, and attaches it to every application.

Can Oporch create or update my profiles on job sites?

Yes. Oporch fills in your profile on Naukri, Internshala, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, Monster, Foundit and Wellfound from the profile you keep in the app.

Does Oporch work for students, freshers and internships?

Yes. Oporch applies to internships and entry-level roles, including on Internshala and Wellfound, and it works just as well for experienced professionals.

Does Oporch work outside India and the United States?

Yes. Your profile can use any country, currency and phone format, and Oporch works with job sites in India and around the world.

Does Oporch run on Mac, Linux or phones?

Oporch runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11, from the Microsoft Store. A Mac version is coming soon. Linux and mobile versions are not available at the moment.

Is Oporch safe to use with my accounts?

Your sign-ins to job sites and email stay on your computer, and Oporch never receives those passwords. It paces its activity to protect your accounts. Some sites restrict automated use, so read our Terms of Use.

Is my data private?

Yes. Oporch does not sell your personal information. It sends your details only where they are needed for the work you ask for: to our servers, to AI providers and to the sites you apply to. Our Privacy Policy has the details.

How does Oporch use AI?

Oporch uses generative AI to answer application questions, write cover letters and profile summaries, and draft emails. Applications may be submitted without you reviewing each answer, and emails are never sent without your approval. Our AI Disclosure explains it in full.

How do I get started?

Download Oporch for Windows (it installs from the Microsoft Store), create a free account, fill in your profile or upload your résumé, and sign in to your job sites inside the app. Then choose your job titles and locations and press Start applying.
